## 摘要
在多路径 TSP 中，给定一个度量和 $k$ 对顶点 $(s_i,t_i)$，其中 $k$ 是输入的一部分，我们要寻找总长度最小的 $s_i$--$t_i$ 路径，使它们共同访问所有顶点。此前，低于 $2$ 的比值仅在图度量和多重仓库（$s_i=t_i$）等特殊情形中已知；在一般度量下，最好的比值是 $1+2e^{-1/2}\approx2.213$，由 Böhm、Friggstad、Mömke 和 Spoerhase 的随机算法给出 [SODA 2025]。我们给出一个确定性算法，相对于其 LP 松弛的一个限制，比值为 $\frac{5e-2}{3e-2}<1.8833$，并将其积分间隙上界设为同一常数。对于有序 TSP，其中一条回路必须按规定的循环顺序访问 $k$ 个给定顶点，我们计算出的回路长度不超过 Armbruster、Mnich 和 Nägele 的 LP 松弛值的 $1+\frac{e}{2(e-1)}<1.7910$ 倍，从而在比值和积分间隙两方面都改进了 $\frac32+\frac1e\approx1.868$。我们的算法从 LP 解中为每一对（对于有序 TSP，为每两个相邻的给定顶点）采样一棵树，但只保留两个端点之间的路径。我们的主要引理表明，对于任意非负边权，将所有顶点连接到这些路径的最便宜森林的期望权重至多为以端点为根的最便宜森林权重的 $1/e$ 倍加上被丢弃分支期望权重的 $(1-1/e)$ 倍。对于多路径 TSP，我们使用 Zhao、Liu 和 Xiao [arXiv 2026] 为多重仓库设计的原始-对偶增长过程的权重；在这些权重下，连接森林还要为校正其度数奇偶性支付部分代价。

## Abstract
In Multi-Path TSP, given a metric and $k$ pairs of vertices $(s_i,t_i)$, where $k$ is part of the input, we seek $s_i$--$t_i$ paths of minimum total length that together visit all vertices. Ratios below $2$ were known only for graphic metrics and special cases such as multiple depots ($s_i=t_i$); in general metrics, the best ratio was $1+2e^{-1/2}\approx2.213$, by a randomized algorithm of Böhm, Friggstad, Mömke and Spoerhase [SODA 2025]. We give a deterministic algorithm with ratio $\frac{5e-2}{3e-2}<1.8833$ relative to a restriction of their LP relaxation, bounding its integrality gap by the same constant. For Ordered TSP, where a tour must visit $k$ given vertices in a prescribed cyclic order, we compute tours within $1+\frac{e}{2(e-1)}<1.7910$ times the value of the LP relaxation of Armbruster, Mnich and Nägele [APPROX 2024], improving $\frac32+\frac1e\approx1.868$ on both the ratio and the integrality gap. Our algorithms sample a tree for every pair (for Ordered TSP, every two consecutive given vertices) from an LP solution, but keep only the path between the two endpoints. Our main lemma shows that, for arbitrary nonnegative edge weights, a cheapest forest connecting all vertices to these paths has expected weight at most $1/e$ times that of a cheapest forest rooted at the endpoints plus $(1-1/e)$ times the expected weight of the discarded branches. For Multi-Path TSP, we use the weights of a primal--dual growth process that Zhao, Liu and Xiao [arXiv 2026] designed for multiple depots; in these weights, the connecting forest also pays for part of correcting its degree parities.
